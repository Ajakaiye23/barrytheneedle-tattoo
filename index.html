05 / QUESTIONS

Before you come in.

How old do I have to be?
Missouri sets the minimum age for tattooing at 18, and government-issued photo ID is checked at the appointment. There are no exceptions to this.
Do I need an appointment?
You can book online through BookedIn, or call (636) 395-7041 to arrange a free consultation first. Consultations carry no obligation to book.
What does it cost?
Sessions are priced by time: $150 for one hour, $300 for two, $400 for three, $500 for four, and $750 for a full day. Consultations are free and an apprentice tattoo is $80.
Are you open on Mondays?
No — the studio is closed Mondays. Tuesday to Saturday it is open 10:00am to 7:00pm, and Sunday 10:00am to 6:00pm.
Can you cover up an existing tattoo?
Yes. Cover-ups are their own craft and we will tell you honestly what is and is not possible with the piece you already have.
How should I prepare?
Eat a proper meal beforehand, skip alcohol the night before, and wear something that gives easy access to the area being worked on.
